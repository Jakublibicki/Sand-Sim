FORECAST SANDBOX / ENVIRONMENTAL MODEL

Every pixel of a source image becomes a grain in a falling-sand simulation. Its colour decides what it is: green grows, blue flows, red burns, grey and brown fall.

The autopilot runs a forecast through rain, fire, earthquake, eruption, impact, surge and vortex, then loses its logic and lets the moss take over. Load a song and the forecast runs for the length of the track, with the spectrum burning along the floor.

Each part of the spectrum drives one kind of event: sub bass shakes the ground, low end feeds the magma, mids spread fire and call lightning, highs bring rain, the air band moves the wind. Drops bring meteors, swells bring surges, and quiet passages let things grow.

Keys 1–9 trigger disasters, 0 loads a new plate, A toggles autopilot, R records, H hides the interface, space pauses, arrow keys skip 5 s. Click the waveform to seek. Click the field to aim fire, eruptions, meteors and vortices. Drop images or an audio file anywhere.
